HanziLock helps you learn Chinese characters without trying: a new hanzi appears on your Lock Screen every day, with its pronunciation (pinyin) and its meaning. You review just by checking the time.

FEATURES

• Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets: the character of the day, always in view
• Full HSK1 deck (~150 words) grouped by theme: pronouns, verbs, numbers, time, family and more
• Flashcards with spaced repetition (6-box Leitner system): every card comes back at the right time
• Progress dashboard: see how many characters you have truly mastered
• Calm interface inspired by Chinese seals
• No ads, no trackers, no account: your progress stays on your device

HANZILOCK PRO

The free version allows 5 reviews per day. The Pro subscription unlocks:
• Unlimited daily reviews
• Detailed progress tracking

Monthly or yearly subscription, auto-renewing, cancel anytime from your Apple ID settings.
