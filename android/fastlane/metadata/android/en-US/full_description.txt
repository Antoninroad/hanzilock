HanziLock helps you learn Chinese characters without trying: a new hanzi appears on your home screen every day, with its pronunciation (pinyin) and its meaning.

FEATURES

• Home screen widget: the character of the day, always in view
• HSK 1 to 6 vocabulary — nearly 5000 characters, sorted by level
• Flashcards with spaced repetition (6-box Leitner system)
• Progress dashboard, level by level
• Daily reminder at a time you choose
• Light / dark theme
• No ads, no trackers, no account: your progress stays on your device

FREE

HSK 1 and 2 (300 words), the widget, 20 reviews per day, the daily reminder.

HANZILOCK PRO

Unlocks all of HSK 1 to 6, unlimited reviews and detailed progress. Monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Cancel anytime in the Play Store.
